Where to Stay Near K-pop Spots

Pick a base by neighborhood: Seongsu for agency and cafe hopping, Hongdae for busking and nightlife, Myeongdong for shopping, Gangnam for the big agencies, Jamsil for concert nights. In Busan, Haeundae is the beach-and-BEXCO base and Seomyeon the transit hub. Each stay below is also pinned on the K-Trail map.

Seoul · Hotel

Hotel The Designers Premier Seongsu & Konkuk

Mid-range hotel between Seongsu-dong and Konkuk University — a few subway stops on Line 2 from the Seongsu agency/cafe cluster (SM Studio Center, KWANGYA@SEONGSU, Onion Seongsu, Daelim Changgo).

Near Konkuk Univ. Station, Gwangjin-gu, Seoul
