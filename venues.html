K-pop Concert Venues in Korea

Where tours and fan events land. Check the artist’s official announcement for dates, entrances and ticketing before you go.

Seoul · Concert / Broadcast

KBS Music Bank Hall

Home of KBS Music Bank tapings, recorded Fridays, at the KBS Annex (별관) building. Check official ticketing/fan-cafe windows before your trip — slots fill fast around comebacks.

359 Yeouidaebang-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Concert / Broadcast

SBS Inkigayo (Prism Tower)

Home of SBS Inkigayo tapings, recorded Sundays, at SBS's Prism Tower broadcast center in Sangam-dong's Digital Media City.

82 Sangamsan-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Concert Venue

Gocheok Sky Dome

Major indoor arena frequently hosting large-scale K-pop concerts and fan events.

430 Gyeongin-ro, Guro-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Concert Venue

KSPO Dome (Olympic Gym)

One of Seoul's most-used concert arenas inside Olympic Park, hosting fan meets and comeback showcases year-round.

Olympic Park, Songpa-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Concert Venue

Jamsil Indoor Stadium

Major indoor arena next to the Jamsil Sports Complex, regularly hosting concerts, fan meetings and award shows.

Jamsil-dong, Songpa-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Concert / Broadcast

CJ ENM Center (Mnet)

Mnet's broadcast center in Sangam DMC, where M Countdown is filmed — right next to SBS's Prism Tower.

66 Sangamsan-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul

Busan · Concert Venue

Busan Asiad Main Stadium

Busan's largest stadium and the city's go-to K-pop mega-concert venue — hosted BTS's free 'Yet To Come' concert in 2022 and the 'ARIRANG' world tour stop in June 2026.

344 World Cup-daero, Geoje-dong, Yeonje-gu, Busan

Busan · Concert / Convention

BEXCO

Busan's main exhibition and convention center — main stage for the annual Busan One Asia Festival (BOF), a K-pop festival co-organized by SM Culture & Contents.

55 APEC-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan

Incheon · Concert / Culture

Sangsang Platform

Converted-warehouse cultural space near Wolmido and Incheon Chinatown — its outdoor plaza hosts the city's own INK (Incheon K-POP) Concert every autumn, drawing a rotating lineup of current idol groups.

33 Wolmi-ro, Jung-gu, Incheon

Incheon · Concert Venue

Inspire Arena

Major arena at the Inspire Entertainment Resort on Yeongjongdo, near Incheon Airport — hosts HYBE's own annual Weverse Con festival plus a steady stream of standalone K-pop concerts (LE SSERAFIM, Xdinary Heroes and more).

127 Gonghangmunhwa-ro, Jung-gu, Incheon

Goyang · Concert Venue

Goyang Stadium

Goyang's main stadium and a fast-rising K-pop mega-concert venue, having hosted G-Dragon, BLACKPINK, and Coldplay stadium shows.

1601 Jungang-ro, Ilsanseo-gu, Goyang

Goyang · Concert / Convention

KINTEX

Korea's largest convention center and a long-running home of KCON Korea, plus major K-pop concerts and fan conventions throughout the year.

217-60 Kintex-ro, Ilsanseo-gu, Goyang
