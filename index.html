About K-Trail Seoul

K-Trail Seoul is an offline-ready pilgrimage map for international K-pop fans visiting Korea, covering Seoul, Busan, Goyang, Incheon and Jeju — agency HQs, concert venues, cafes, culture spots, and now nearby stays and eats too. Every location is researched from public sources and cross-checked across 2+ sources where possible, but Korea's K-pop map moves fast (agencies relocate, venues change) — always verify hours, access and current addresses before relying on it to travel.

Tip: on the Map tab, the download icon next to Search saves that view's map tiles for offline use — handy before you lose signal underground, on the subway, or abroad. It only saves what's on screen, not the whole city, to stay within OpenStreetMap's usage policy.

Found something outdated or missing? This map keeps growing — more Stay/Eat coverage and additional cities are planned.
