About this prototype

This is an early skeleton for K-Trail Seoul — an offline-ready pilgrimage map for international K-pop fans visiting Seoul. Content shown here is placeholder sample data, not the final curated list.

Planned: real researched locations with verified addresses and credited photos, offline map caching for use without wifi, and deep links back to each group's dedicated site on the K-Wire Network.
