Privacy

K-Trail Seoul keeps this simple. Last updated Nov 2026.

What the app stores on your device

Your saved spots, language and theme choices are kept in your own browser storage. They never leave your device, and there are no accounts or sign-ups.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are useful. It may set cookies and collect anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an extension.

Third-party services

Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, the map library from cdnjs, and fonts from Google Fonts. Directions and some links open Google Maps or other sites, which have their own privacy policies. When you tap them, those services receive your request directly.

Offline map tiles

The download button caches the map tiles on screen in your browser so they work without a connection. You can clear them from your browser's site data.

Questions or corrections: message us on Pinterest at @ktrailseoul or on Instagram at @kpop_lowdown.
