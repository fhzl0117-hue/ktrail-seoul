K-pop Agency Headquarters in Korea

Agency buildings are working offices: most are exterior photo spots only, with no public entry. Agencies relocate every few years, so confirm the current address before you travel.

Seoul · Agency (exterior)

HYBE Headquarters

Working HQ for BTS, TXT, ENHYPEN, LE SSERAFIM, BOYNEXTDOOR and more — exterior photo spot only, no public entry. The HYBE Insight museum here closed in Jan 2023 and has not confirmed a Seoul reopening — check official channels before planning a museum visit.

42 Hangang-daero, Yongsan-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Agency (exterior)

SM Entertainment HQ (D-Tower)

SM's current headquarters near Seoul Forest, after moving from Gangnam. Distinctive facade is a popular exterior photo spot — no public tours of the building itself.

83-21 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Agency (exterior)

JYP Entertainment HQ

JYP's headquarters in Seongnae-dong, near Gangdong-gu Office Station — the company scrapped a planned move to a new Godeok-dong building in Oct 2026 and is staying put, expanding into neighboring buildings instead. Exterior photo spot only.

17 Seongnae-ro, Gangdong-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Agency (exterior)

YG Entertainment HQ

YG's Hapjeong headquarters, expanded with a newer building alongside the original. The exterior and logo sign are a regular stop on fan walking routes.

7 Huiujeong-ro 1-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Agency (exterior)

SM Entertainment Studio Center

SM's production/recording studio in Gangnam, separate from the Seongsu HQ — a long-running fan gathering spot for fansigns and comeback promotions.

114 Seolleung-ro 190-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Agency (exterior)

ADOR

42 Hangang-daero, Yongsan-gu, Seoul

Seoul · Agency (exterior)
